# Nizlo MSP operations resources

Free, practical checklists for managed service providers and lean IT teams evaluating, deploying, operating, migrating, and offboarding remote monitoring and management software.

Every resource below is public, ungated, and written to be usable independently of Nizlo's product. The canonical guides include downloadable field templates where available.

Start with the [free RMM calculators collection](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/rmm-calculators.html) to model migration cost and break-even, compare public prices, evaluate common pricing structures, and build an ROI case without an account or email gate.

Measure alert fatigue with the [RMM alert-noise baseline worksheet](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/rmm-alert-noise-baseline-worksheet.html), a public 23-field CSV and 14-day before-and-after method for separating raw events, unique incidents, duplicates, false positives, actionable work, after-hours load, acknowledgement time, and resolution time.

Define buying criteria before vendor demos with the [RMM requirements matrix and RFP worksheet](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/rmm-requirements-matrix-template.html), a public 32-requirement, 18-field CSV for priorities, evidence requests, pilot tests, acceptance criteria, owned gaps, remediation commitments, and decisions.

## Source-dated pricing dataset

The [public RMM pricing dataset](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/public-rmm-pricing-dataset.html) records fourteen published price disclosures and normalizes exact public rates to one technician and fleets of 10, 50, 100, and 500 endpoints. Download the [CSV snapshot](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/data/public-rmm-pricing-comparison-2026-11-02.csv), consume its [Schema.org and DCAT JSON-LD metadata](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/data/public-rmm-pricing-comparison-2026-11-02.jsonld), verify every row against its linked vendor source, and use the maintained [calculator and methodology](https://nizlo.com/guides/cheapest-rmm-for-small-msps) for context. Numeric cells are left blank when official public pages require a quote or do not support exact scenario math. Nizlo maintains the dataset and is one of the compared products; the rows are subscription math and pricing-visibility observations, not an independent endorsement or a feature-equivalence claim.

## Reusable field templates

Browse the public [RMM operations and incident response template library](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/templates/) for direct downloads and usage guidance.

Use the [RMM vendor due-diligence evidence register](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/rmm-vendor-due-diligence-evidence-register.html) to turn thirty security, operations, privacy, incident-response, commercial, and exit questions into a documented review. The [17-field CSV](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/templates/rmm-vendor-due-diligence-evidence-register.csv) records evidence status, references, findings, risk, remediation owners, exception approval, and renewal review without requiring an account.

Use the [RMM alert escalation matrix](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/rmm-alert-escalation-matrix-template.html) to map 27 example alert rules to confirmation windows, owners, response and acknowledgement targets, escalation paths, customer notifications, suppression conditions, evidence, closure tests, and false-positive review. The [25-field CSV](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/templates/rmm-alert-escalation-matrix.csv) is public and ungated; the maintained [RMM alert escalation policy guide](https://nizlo.com/guides/rmm-alert-escalation-policy-template) explains how to adapt and operationally test it before production use.

Use the [RMM alert noise reduction checklist](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/rmm-alert-noise-reduction-checklist.html) to baseline alert load, define actionable conditions, correlate duplicates, route ownership, govern suppression, and measure the effect of each tuning change. The maintained [RMM alert-tuning guide](https://nizlo.com/guides/rmm-alert-tuning-checklist) provides the broader operating workflow.

Use the [MSP backup restore test checklist](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/msp-backup-restore-test-checklist.html) to run a controlled recovery drill across authorization, recovery-point selection, isolated restore execution, byte-level integrity where applicable, permissions, application usability, timing, exception ownership, safe cleanup, and closure. It links to the maintained [Nizlo backup overview](https://nizlo.com/backup) and [RMM with backup guide](https://nizlo.com/solutions/rmm-with-backup) while remaining vendor-neutral.

Use the [Windows patch failure triage checklist](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/windows-patch-failure-triage-checklist.html) when a Windows update reports failed, remains applicable, or leaves an uncertain endpoint state. Its 24 checks preserve the original error, reconcile affected scope, test prerequisites and servicing health, define safe remediation, validate the post-change state, and turn unresolved devices into owned time-bounded exceptions.

Use the dedicated [RMM deployment-readiness checklist template](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/rmm-deployment-readiness-template.html) for phase guidance, field documentation, and the public 33-control CSV. It helps assign owners, record evidence, define rollback triggers, and reconcile exceptions across governance, inventory, preparation, pilot, wave rollout, verification, and closeout.

Use the [RMM change-window and rollback-plan template](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/rmm-change-window-rollback-plan.html) when a deployment, migration, policy change, or automation rollout needs explicit timing, go/no-go authority, success criteria, communications, and a reversible execution path. The underlying [26-control CSV](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/templates/rmm-change-window-rollback-plan.csv) is public and ungated. The maintained [RMM maintenance-window guide](https://nizlo.com/guides/rmm-maintenance-window-template) explains how to define scope, timing, notices, suppression, validation, exception ownership, and rollback before an endpoint change begins.

Use the [RMM incident response runbook template](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/rmm-incident-response-runbook-template.html) to coordinate readiness, detection, evidence preservation, containment, investigation, recovery, stakeholder communication, and corrective action after a suspected RMM security event. The underlying [30-control CSV](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/templates/rmm-incident-response-runbook.csv) is public and ungated, and the canonical [RMM incident response checklist](https://nizlo.com/guides/rmm-incident-response-checklist) explains the full eight-stage workflow.

Use the [RMM migration checklist workbook](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/rmm-migration-workbook-template.html) to reconcile source inventory, target mappings, pilot evidence, coexistence, cutover waves, rollback, source retirement, and final acceptance. The underlying [30-control CSV](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/templates/rmm-migration-workbook.csv) is public and ungated.

Use the [RMM migration cost and break-even calculator](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/rmm-migration-cost-calculator.html) to model internal labor, implementation fees, parallel subscriptions, retained tools, recurring cost change, first-year impact, and payback time. The formulas are published on the page, the example does not prefill Nizlo pricing, and all figures stay in the browser.

Use the [RMM evaluation scorecard](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/rmm-evaluation-scorecard-template.html) to compare observed pilot evidence across inventory, monitoring, patching, automation, remote support, permissions, audit evidence, recovery, removal, and technician usability. The underlying [10-area CSV](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/templates/rmm-evaluation-scorecard.csv) is public and ungated.

Use the [RMM quote comparison worksheet](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/rmm-quote-comparison-worksheet.html) to normalize dated vendor proposals across platform fees, minimum commitments, billing units, included technicians and endpoints, mandatory add-ons, implementation, training, first-year totals, renewal uplifts, termination terms, and exit costs. The underlying [26-field CSV](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/templates/rmm-quote-comparison-worksheet.csv) is public and ungated.

Use the [RMM first-endpoint acceptance checklist](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/rmm-first-endpoint-acceptance-checklist.html) to prove identity, alert routing, authorized actions, patch reconciliation, and clean removal before a pilot expands. The underlying [five-gate CSV](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/templates/rmm-first-endpoint-acceptance.csv) records success criteria, evidence, stop conditions, ownership, and status without requiring an account.

Use the [Windows patch exception review and closure template](https://izikipizi.github.io/nizlo-msp-operations-resources/windows-patch-exception-review-template.html) when an update cannot follow the normal deployment schedule.
