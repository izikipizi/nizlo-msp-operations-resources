  5. Require explicit customer and provider acceptance before steady-state support begins.

MSP onboarding timeline questions

How long should MSP client onboarding take?

There is no universal duration. Use the contracted scope, endpoint count, access readiness, customer response time, migration work, and acceptance requirements to set realistic phase lengths.

Why use business days?

The calculator skips Saturdays and Sundays so the milestones align with a typical working schedule. It does not know local holidays, blackout dates, or customer-specific calendars.

Does reaching the last date mean the customer is live?

No. A date is a planning checkpoint. Go-live requires the named evidence, resolved or accepted exceptions, operational ownership, and explicit acceptance.
