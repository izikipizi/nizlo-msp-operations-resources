What the exercise tests

Independent command

Coordinate when the RMM console or normal collaboration channel is assumed unavailable or untrusted.

Scope and evidence

Reconcile tenants, endpoints, identities, integrations, automations, timestamps, missing logs, and explicitly tracked unknowns.

Containment decisions

Balance immediate risk, critical service dependencies, reversible actions, authorization, and preserved evidence.

Recovery and closure

Restore in bounded waves, reconcile inventory, keep corrective actions visible, and schedule evidence-backed retesting.

How to run it safely

  1. Name the exercise sponsor, facilitator, observers, technical leads, business owners, and stop authority.
  2. Define participating tenants and systems, out-of-scope boundaries, assumptions, and production-safety stop conditions.
  3. Use the injects in sequence or select only those that match the exercise objectives. Keep all production actions simulated unless separately approved.
  4. Timebox each decision and require participants to name authority, evidence, customer impact, rollback, and the next update.
  5. Score observable outcomes against the CSV pass criteria. Assign every gap an owner, due date, closure evidence, and retest date.

This template is an operational exercise aid. It is not legal advice, forensic guidance, a compliance certification, or authorization to access or change production systems.

Minimum exercise record

Preserve the approved scope, participant roster, assumptions, injects used, decisions, decision authority, timestamps, evidence references, communication outputs, pass or fail rationale, unresolved gaps, owners, due dates, and retest results. Keep secrets, customer data, restricted reports, and sensitive exports in approved evidence storage; record only authorized references in the worksheet.

Tabletop exercise FAQ

What should the exercise test?

Test independent command channels, privileged identity decisions, tenant and endpoint scope, automation containment, evidence preservation, customer communications, staged recovery, inventory reconciliation, corrective-action ownership, and retesting.

Should participants make production changes?

No. Keep the exercise simulated unless a separately approved and bounded technical test exists. Define stop conditions before the exercise and prevent participants from improvising production actions.

How should the exercise be scored?

Score each objective against observable evidence and explicit pass criteria. Record gaps, owners, due dates, and retest requirements instead of passing the exercise by consensus alone.
