Set the denominator first

Agree which customers, sites, services, users, endpoints, and dates are in scope. Keep stale devices, exclusions, pauses, and unmeasurable records visible instead of removing inconvenient outcomes.

Separate operational clocks

Acknowledgement, service restoration, and final resolution answer different questions. Report each against its approved clock rules and preserve the records behind the percentage.

Turn trends into decisions

Ticket, alert, patch, incident, automation, project, and lifecycle trends should end with an explicit decision: remediate, accept risk, change scope, fund work, or collect missing evidence.

Close with an action register

Every action needs a named provider or customer owner, a due date, a dependency, a success definition, and an evidence reference. Reconcile the register at the next review before opening new work.

Safe use:

The checklist is an operational planning aid, not a promise of service levels, security, compliance, availability, recovery, or business outcomes. Replace every example definition with terms that match the signed agreement, supported tooling, authoritative source records, and approved risk process.

Reporting boundary:

An RMM can contribute endpoint, alert, patch, automation, remote-support, and reporting evidence. Ticketing, satisfaction, projects, backup and restore, security findings, commercial scope, and business decisions may come from separate systems. Do not imply that one console proves every QBR item.

MSP QBR template questions

What belongs in an MSP quarterly business review?

Use agreed scope, source-backed service and risk metrics, prior-action status, endpoint and security evidence, project and lifecycle decisions, and a named next-quarter action register.

How should SLA performance be reported?

Keep acknowledgement, restoration, and resolution separate. Preserve clock pauses, exclusions, breaches, and unmeasurable records so the result can be reproduced.

Should stale endpoints stay in patch reporting?

Yes, while they remain authorized and in scope. Show failed, pending-restart, excepted, and unknown or stale states separately.

Is the template tied to one platform?

No. Populate it from the systems and approved registers that actually hold the evidence.
