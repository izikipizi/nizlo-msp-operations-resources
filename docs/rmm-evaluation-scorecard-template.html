Score observed behavior

Use 0 for failed or unacceptable risk, 1 for partial, ambiguous, or manual behavior, and 2 for reliable and controlled behavior backed by evidence.

Cover the operating lifecycle

Evaluate identity, monitoring, patching, automation, remote support, permissions, audit evidence, recovery, removal, and technician usability.

Do not average away failure

A high total cannot neutralize a failed tenant-boundary, recovery, removal, or other rollout-critical control. Resolve the risk and repeat the test.

Keep the decision auditable

Link evidence without pasting secrets, assign each open risk, set a retest date, and record a go, revise, or stop decision.

Suggested interpretation: after all ten areas are scored, 16–20 is a strong result, 12–15 is mixed, and 0–11 is weak—but any zero should block rollout until the underlying risk is resolved and retested.
