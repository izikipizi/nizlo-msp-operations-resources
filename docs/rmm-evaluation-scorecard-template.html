RMM evaluation scorecard FAQ

What criteria belong in an RMM evaluation scorecard?

Cover endpoint identity and inventory, monitoring and alerting, patching, automation, remote support, permissions, audit evidence, recovery, removal, and technician usability. Define the expected result and evidence before each test.

How should RMM products be scored?

Use the same 0–2 scale for every product. Do not let a high total hide a failed tenant-boundary, recovery, removal, or other rollout-critical control.

What evidence should an RMM comparison require?

Keep dated screenshots, exports, logs, ticket or alert records, expected and observed results, defects, risk owners, remediation, and retest outcomes. Link evidence without placing credentials or secrets in the scorecard.

When is an RMM trial ready for a go/no-go decision?

Decide after planned tests are complete, critical failures are resolved and retested, open risks have owners, removal has been tested, and an authorized decision maker has reviewed the evidence.
