Published formulas

Current monthly cost = current licenses + (current admin hours × loaded labor rate)

Proposed monthly cost = consolidated platform + retained tools + add-ons + (proposed admin hours × loaded labor rate)

Transition cost = (current recurring software × overlap months) + (migration hours × loaded labor rate) + external fees

Payback months = transition cost ÷ positive monthly savings

Net savings = (monthly savings × period) − transition cost

Consolidation is not automatic replacement.

This calculator performs arithmetic only. It does not prove that one product replaces an RMM, PSA, EDR, backup platform, MDM, documentation system, security tool, or integration. Confirm customer separation, operating-system support, monitoring, patching, automation, remote access, service-desk workflow, security controls, export, support, and exit requirements in writing and in a representative pilot.

Relationship disclosure: Nizlo maintains this vendor-neutral calculator and sells RMM software. The tool does not prefill Nizlo pricing or promise that Nizlo—or any vendor—will produce the savings shown.

Keep unproven tools in the comparison

Suppose your current stack costs $600 per month and the replacement suite costs $430. You can verify its RMM workflow in the pilot, but you still need your $120 backup service and $80 ticketing tool. Enter those under retained tools. The proposed stack costs $630 per month before labor and one-time fees, or $30 more than the current stack.

Test an actual file restore and a complete ticket from alert to closure before removing those costs from a second scenario. Record the result in the RMM evaluation scorecard and keep both totals until the people responsible approve the change.
