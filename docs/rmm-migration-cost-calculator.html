Transparent formulas
  • monthly savings = current cost − target cost − retained tools
  • one-time cost = labor hours × loaded rate + fees + overlap months × (current + target + retained)
  • 12-month impact = monthly savings × 12 − one-time cost
  • 12-month migration ROI = 12-month impact ÷ one-time cost × 100
  • 36-month impact = monthly savings × 36 − one-time cost
  • break-even months = one-time cost ÷ monthly savings, only when monthly savings are positive

RMM ROI and migration-cost questions

How do you calculate RMM migration ROI?

This model divides the 12-month net impact by the one-time migration cost and multiplies by 100. It keeps recurring subscription savings separate from the migration investment.

What should be included in RMM migration cost?

Include internal labor at a loaded rate, implementation or consulting fees, contract exit costs, training, and any period when both platforms are billed.

Does a positive ROI estimate prove a migration will succeed?

No. Validate feature coverage, security controls, integrations, support, data export, platform compatibility, and observed pilot results before deciding.
