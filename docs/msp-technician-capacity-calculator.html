Published formulas

Tickets = endpoints × tickets per endpoint per month

Reactive hours = tickets × average handling minutes ÷ 60

Adjusted reactive hours = reactive hours × (1 + rework buffer)

Usable support hours per tech = (productive hours − non-ticket hours) × (1 − target headroom)

Required FTE = adjusted reactive hours ÷ usable support hours per tech

Supported endpoints = current technicians × usable support hours per tech ÷ adjusted reactive hours per endpoint

This is a capacity model, not a staffing benchmark.

Endpoint-to-technician ratios are descriptive outputs, not universal targets. Two teams with the same endpoint count may have different capacity because of customer complexity, user behavior, automation, skill mix, after-hours coverage, projects, onboarding, security events, travel, leave, vendor coordination, and data quality.

Use a representative period and compare the result with queue age, reopen rate, SLA performance, backlog, overtime, customer experience, and technician health. Do not use the estimate alone to reduce staffing or promise a service level.
