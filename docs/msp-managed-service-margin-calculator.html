Price a repair shop’s first managed-service customer

In this fictional pilot, a shop agrees to monitor ten authorized business workstations, review alerts, and check patches during an agreed window. Emergency callouts and project work are quoted separately.

Half an hour of monthly labor at $60 per hour plus $18 in allocated tools costs $48 per month. An $80 monthly base fee leaves $32 of modeled contribution, or 40%, before unentered costs. If labor doubles to one hour, cost rises to $78 and the same quote leaves $2. Use the pilot to measure recurring work and include any admin, coverage, or shared costs your service needs to recover.

Published formulas

Revenue = endpoints × endpoint price + clients × client base fee

Included cost = endpoints × (RMM + security + other device tools) + endpoints × delivery minutes ÷ 60 × loaded labor rate + clients × client overhead + fixed costs

Modeled margin = (revenue − included cost) ÷ revenue

Break-even endpoint price = (included cost − client base-fee revenue) ÷ endpoints

Target endpoint price = (included cost ÷ (1 − target margin) − client base-fee revenue) ÷ endpoints

This is a pricing model, not accounting or financial advice.

Accounting definitions of gross margin and direct cost differ. This page reports a modeled contribution margin using only the costs entered. It does not include taxes, sales and marketing, bad debt, churn, utilization changes, escalation spikes, after-hours work, warranty obligations, financing, or scope creep unless you include them in an input.

Relationship disclosure: Nizlo maintains this vendor-neutral tool. It does not prefill Nizlo pricing or claim that Nizlo—or any other vendor—will produce the margin shown.
