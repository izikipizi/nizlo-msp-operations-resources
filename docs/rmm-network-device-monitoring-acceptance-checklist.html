Minimum production gate

  • The approved device denominator is reconciled and every target has a durable identity and accountable owner.
  • The polling dependency, credential lifecycle, freshness semantics, alert route, outage, recovery, and data-gap behavior have been tested.
  • Every conditional result has a compensating control, due date, owner, and approver.

Network-monitoring checklist questions

What must be tested?Scope, inventory, pollers, protocol security, identity, objects, freshness, alerts, dependencies, outages, recovery, and ownership.
Does discovery prove monitoring?No. Discovery is only observation; acceptance requires fresh metrics, routing, tested failures, and evidence.
Should an MSP use SNMPv3?Evaluate authenticated and privacy-protected SNMPv3 where supported; formally govern any weaker exception.
What blocks rollout?Unknown scope, unmanaged credentials, untested dependencies, stale data shown healthy, ownerless alerts, or failed outage and recovery tests.
Is this Nizlo-specific?No. It evaluates any platform, including whether Nizlo's current lack of SNMP and network discovery should disqualify it.
Relationship and capability disclosure: Nizlo maintains this vendor-neutral checklist. Nizlo does not currently claim SNMP or network-discovery support. Use the checklist to evaluate Nizlo or another platform and to decide whether dedicated network monitoring must remain in the toolset. It is an operational starting point, not a security certification, penetration test, vendor guarantee, or substitute for device-vendor guidance.
