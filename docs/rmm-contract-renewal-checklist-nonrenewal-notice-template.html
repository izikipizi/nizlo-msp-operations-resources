RMM contract renewal checklist

Record the source, owner, decision, and evidence for every step. A checked box without the controlling document or delivery proof is not closure.

1

Assemble the controlling documents

Collect the executed agreement, order forms, amendments, renewal notices, invoices, and relevant correspondence. Identify which terms control if documents conflict.

2

Identify the renewal date

Record the service start, current term, renewal or end date, and automatic renewal term from the controlling documents—not from memory or a sales page.

3

Verify the notice rule

Confirm the notice period, calendar or business day definition, time zone, holidays, and whether notice is effective when sent or only when received.

4

Verify notice delivery

Identify every permitted delivery method, recipient, address, portal, required wording, tracking method, and acknowledgement requirement.

5

Reconcile the complete price

Capture base fees, minimums, technicians, endpoints, required add-ons, implementation, taxes, credits, billing cadence, and renewal uplift language.

6

Review operating evidence

Evaluate service history, support, incidents, security, patching, remote access, reporting, integrations, automation, and technician experience.

7

Inventory data and exports

List device data, scripts, policies, audit records, reports, credentials, integrations, documentation, and configurations that must be exported or retained.

8

Review transition obligations

Confirm assistance, export formats, retention, deletion, access revocation, agent removal, fees, and timing after termination or non-renewal.

9

Select the decision path

Record renew, renegotiate, evaluate alternatives, or exit; name the owner and approver; and state the evidence and assumptions behind the decision.

10

Prepare the operational plan

If changing platforms, define pilot, coexistence, rollback, cutover, endpoint reconciliation, source retirement, billing closure, and acceptance.

11

Obtain qualified review

Have the authorized commercial owner and qualified counsel verify contract interpretation, authority, deadline, delivery requirements, and final wording where needed.

12

Deliver and preserve proof

Send through every required channel before the verified deadline. Retain approval, final notice, timestamp, recipient, tracking, receipt, acknowledgement, and follow-up.

Non-renewal notice starting point

Replace every bracketed field. Do not send this text until an authorized owner and qualified counsel have confirmed it matches the signed agreement and applicable requirements.

Subject: Notice of non-renewal — [agreement or account identifier]

To: [contractually required recipient]

This notice is provided by [legal customer name] regarding [agreement name and identifier], dated [agreement date], including applicable order forms and amendments.

Pursuant to [specific agreement section], [legal customer name] provides notice that it does not intend to renew the agreement beyond [renewal or end date]. This notice is intended to satisfy the required [number]-day notice period.

Please confirm receipt in writing and identify the authorized contact and schedule for transition assistance, data export, access revocation, agent removal, final invoicing, data retention or deletion, and account closure required under the agreement.

Nothing in this notice waives any right, remedy, obligation, dispute, or requirement under the agreement or applicable law. [Retain, revise, or remove this sentence only with qualified review.]

Authorized sender:
[Name]
[Title]
[Legal customer name]
[Address and contact details]
[Date and time sent]

RMM renewal and notice questions

What should an RMM renewal review include?

Review the controlling documents, renewal date, notice rules, delivery method, complete price, operating evidence, exports, transition assistance, termination fees, access removal, approvals, and proof.

Can I send the sample notice as written?

No. It is an operational starting point, not legal advice. Replace every placeholder and obtain authorized commercial and qualified legal review where needed.

What proof should be retained?

Retain the approved final notice, timestamp, channel, recipient, tracking or portal confirmation, receipt, acknowledgement, and follow-up correspondence.
