Formula: verified installed ÷ (managed endpoints − approved exclusions). The outcome-state total must equal the denominator before the percentage is reportable.
Reporting boundary: record the customer/site, required update baseline, reporting timestamp, maximum evidence age, approved exclusions, and source system beside the result.
Do not reward missing data: an offline, stale, or disappeared endpoint stays unresolved until it is reconnected, formally retired, or moved through an approved exception process.
