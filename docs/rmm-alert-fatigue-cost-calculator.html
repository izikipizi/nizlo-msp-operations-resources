Published formulas

Generated alerts = alerts per day × measured days

Reviewed alerts = generated alerts × human-review rate

Review hours = reviewed alerts × minutes per alert ÷ 60

Noise-review cost = reviewed alerts × measured noise share × minutes per alert ÷ 60 × loaded labor rate

Recoverable hours = noise-review hours × bounded noise-reduction target

Do not optimize cost by hiding failures.

Reduced alert volume is useful only when critical detection, confirmed incidents, acknowledgement time, recovery evidence, after-hours coverage, and escalation tests remain acceptable. Track suppressed alerts separately, set hard expiry on maintenance suppression, and retest changes on representative endpoints.
