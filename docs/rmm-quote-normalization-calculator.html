    Transparent formulas
    • endpoint charge = max(0, endpoints − included endpoints) × endpoint rate
    • technician charge = max(0, technicians − included technicians) × extra-technician rate
    • add-ons = endpoints × required add-on rate + other required monthly fees
    • pre-discount monthly = max(monthly minimum, base + endpoint charge + technician charge + add-ons)
    • recurring monthly = pre-discount monthly × (1 − discount %)
    • contract cost = recurring monthly × contract months + one-time fees
    • effective monthly = contract cost ÷ contract months

    RMM quote-normalization questions

    How do you normalize an RMM quote?

    Separate the base fee, included quantities, overage rates, add-ons, minimum, discount, one-time fees, and term. Compare the resulting recurring, first-year, contract, and per-endpoint costs.

    Should implementation fees be included?

    Keep one-time fees visible and allocate them across the term only as a comparison aid. Do not disguise them as recurring subscription cost.

    Does the lowest normalized quote win?

    No. Verify operating-system coverage, security, integrations, support, retained tools, export, migration, and pilot evidence before selecting a platform.
