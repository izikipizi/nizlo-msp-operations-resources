RMM pricing and contract questions

Does a monthly RMM price mean there is no annual contract?

No. A monthly figure can be an annual-billing equivalent. Record billing cadence, initial term, renewal term, cancellation notice, and early-termination terms separately.

Does unlimited-endpoint RMM mean there is no device minimum?

Not necessarily. Unlimited endpoints describes how usage is counted after purchase; it does not prove there is no platform, technician, site, or minimum-spend requirement.

What RMM contract terms should an MSP compare?

Compare the pricing unit, minimum quantity or spend, included technicians and endpoints, required add-ons, implementation, billing cadence, initial term, auto-renewal, uplift language, cancellation, termination, export, agent removal, and exit costs.

Find the price a renewal cap applies to

Suppose a fictional quote lists $100 per month, discounts the first 12 months to $80 per month, and adds a $200 setup fee. Year one costs $1,160. If the renewal price is set at the full 5% cap, the calculation depends on which rate the agreement uses.

  • At 5% above the $80 discounted rate, year two costs $1,008 ($84 per month), for $2,168 over two years.
  • At 5% above the $100 list rate, year two costs $1,260 ($105 per month), for $2,420 over two years.

The two interpretations differ by $252 before tax. Ask the vendor to identify the baseline in writing and confirm whether add-ons and minimum commitments are covered by the cap. Record the confirmed rate and the notice deadline in the worksheet. Use the RMM contract checklist to review renewal and exit terms alongside cost.

Reconcile the count that goes on the invoice

At $2.50 per endpoint and a 100-endpoint minimum, a vendor export showing 108 billable records produces a $270 monthly estimate. If eight records are retired or duplicates and the vendor confirms that removing them lowers the billable count before its cutoff, the estimate drops to $250.
