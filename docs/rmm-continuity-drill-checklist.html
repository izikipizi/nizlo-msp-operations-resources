Prove your MSP can operate when the RMM cannot.

Run a bounded, evidence-led exercise for loss of the RMM console, API, agent communications, alerting, automation, or remote access—without improvising unsafe production changes.

Download the CSVUse the incident tabletopReview exit readiness

Authorize and bound

Name the authority, scope, prohibited actions, safety exclusions, stop conditions, and evidence owner before the clock starts.

Work independently

Test contacts, inventories, emergency identities, runbooks, ticket intake, approved endpoint access, and telemetry that do not depend on the affected RMM.

Recover by function

Verify console, API, agent, alerting, automation, and remote-access recovery separately. A vendor status page alone is not acceptance evidence.

Reconcile and retest

Own queued jobs, stale alerts, duplicate records, missed changes, temporary access, corrective actions, due dates, and a documented retest.

Safe-use boundary: This is an operational exercise aid, not incident-response, legal, compliance, or security advice. Prefer simulation unless an approved production test is necessary. Do not create bypass accounts, weaken controls, or access customer systems without documented authority.
Relationship disclosure: Nizlo, a Windows-first RMM and IT-management platform, maintains this vendor-neutral checklist. It can be used with any RMM and does not claim that Nizlo or another vendor passes these controls.
