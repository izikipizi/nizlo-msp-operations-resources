RMM deployment readiness checklist

Wave rollout · 4 controlsSet wave boundaries, reconcile every result, record exceptions, and require explicit go/no-go decisions.
Verification and closeout · 8 controlsConfirm coverage, health, policy state, access, documentation, exception ownership, evidence, and final approval.

How to use it

  1. Import the CSV into a spreadsheet, ticket, or change-management record.
  2. Replace generic owners, dates, and rollback triggers with named people and measurable thresholds.
  3. Complete the pilot controls before approving a wider wave.
  4. Reconcile successful, failed, excluded, offline, and unknown endpoints after every wave.
  5. Keep exceptions open until each has an owner, due date, evidence, and disposition.

The checklist is an operational planning aid. It does not replace your organization’s security, backup, change-control, or incident-response requirements.

Field reference

Each row includes a phase, stable control ID, control, owner, status, evidence, exception owner, due date, rollback trigger, and notes. The Data Package descriptor publishes the field schema, byte count, and SHA-256 hash for automated validation.
