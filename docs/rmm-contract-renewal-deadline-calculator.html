RMM renewal deadline questions

How is the non-renewal deadline calculated?

This tool subtracts the notice period from the renewal or end date using calendar days. The agreement may define timing differently, so verify the result.

Why set an earlier internal deadline?

The buffer creates time to collect evidence, compare alternatives, approve the decision, plan negotiation or migration, and deliver notice correctly.

Is this legal advice?

No. It is planning arithmetic. The signed agreement and applicable law control; use qualified counsel for interpretation.
