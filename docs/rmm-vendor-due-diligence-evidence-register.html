Record the exact report, configuration, audit trail, contract clause, export, or pilot result that supports each response.

Separate gaps from accepted risk

Give every shortfall an owner and due date. Require named, time-bounded approval for exceptions instead of leaving blanks.

Test the exit before signing

Validate exports, agent removal, data deletion, and administrative access revocation while the vendor still has an incentive to help, then preserve the evidence and owners in the RMM vendor exit plan.

Schedule the next review now

Evidence expires. Record a renewal review date for assurance reports, subprocessors, pricing, access controls, and response terms.

Safe use: this register is an operational starting point, not legal, regulatory, or audit advice. Tailor questions to your data, customers, contracts, jurisdictions, and risk tolerance.
